About Expense Tracker

Smart, Secure and Simple Expense Management for Everyday Life.

Who We Are

Expense Tracker is a personal finance management application that helps users monitor income, expenses, savings and budgets in one secure place. Whether you're a student, employee or business owner, our application helps you understand where your money goes and make smarter financial decisions.

Our Features

📊 Expense Summary

Get an overview of your income, expenses, savings and available balance.

📅 Monthly Reports

Analyze your monthly spending patterns with detailed reports.

📈 Budget Planning

Create budgets and stay within your spending limits.
